# MF-0069 evidence: gate:planning requires a plan to declare its parsers

- New rule `checkPlanParsers` in `scripts/lib/planning-check.mjs`, run by `pnpm gate:planning` (CI and the Stop gate).
- **Who it judges.** Tickets after MF-0068, not reserved, at stage `build`/`test`/`handoff`/`watch`.
- **What triggers it.** Any task `owns` glob that may cover a non-test file under `scripts/`, `.claude/hooks/` or
  `src/engine/`. Matching is exact (braces expanded, MF-0048 `globsOverlap`), and a glob it cannot expand counts as
  in territory.
- **What the plan needs.** A `## Parsers` section that says `None: <reason>`, or holds one `### <name>` block per
  parser. Each block has three labels, each opening its own line outside any fence:
  - `Accepted grammar:`
  - `Rejected inputs`, with a table of at least 3 rows before the next label
  - `Exempt-path test:`
- **Template.** The `## Parsers` template in `.claude/skills/gsd-goal-loop/references/templates.md` now has this shape,
  with an example between `<!-- parsers-example -->` markers that a test checks against the rule.
- **Proof case.** MF-0048's shipped LOOP-PLAN, judged as a governed ticket, gives
  `MF-9999: LOOP-PLAN owns .claude/hooks/lib/agent-scopes.mjs but has no "## Parsers" section (write "None: <reason>"
  or one ### block per parser)`.
- **Impact today.** Nothing reddens. No ticket after MF-0068 is past intake on any of 54 refs, except MF-0069 itself,
  whose plan passes (reviewer probe).
- **Design changes from the draft (user, 2026-11-09).**
  - The draft's plan-text regex was dropped: it fired on 45 of 54 shipped plans and missed the proof case.
  - The rule lives in gate:planning rather than in a separate `ticket:plan-lint` CLI.
